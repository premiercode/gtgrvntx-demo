02Mile 02 / Rentals

Rentals kept road‑ready by our own shop.

“Our on site repair center is here to oversee our rental fleet and service all of our customers!”

4.9

Outdoorsy Superhost rating from 150+ reviews on Outdoorsy, as of Nov 2026

It started in 2019 with the owners’ own Super C motorhome. Today the fleet runs from Class A diesel pushers and Class Cs to Sprinter vans, a fifth wheel, a toy hauler, travel trailers and a tent trailer.

Your trip, mile by mile

  1. Book

    2-night minimum. Renters 25 and up. Insurance is bought at booking.

  2. Get the welcome kit

    A welcome letter, instructions and YouTube how-to videos for your RV.

  3. Pick up

    From 10:00 AM, with kitchen kit, towels, hoses, power cord and leveling blocks.

  4. Hit the road

    150 miles per day included, $0.45 per mile after.

  5. Roll back in

    Return by 4:00 PM. You’re good to go again.

The fine print, in plain words

  • No pets, no smoking.
  • Bedding is a $45 add-on.
  • Insurance at booking covers comprehensive, collision and liability, with a $2,500 maximum deductible.
  • Towables: you bring the tow vehicle (class 3 hitch, brake controller, 7-pin). A weight-distribution hitch is provided.
